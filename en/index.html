Questions we get asked

Is the mock-up really free, with no commitment?

Yes. You receive your personalised mock-up within 48 business hours without paying or signing anything. If it doesn't win you over, that's the end of it.

Do we need to meet?

No. Everything happens remotely: your online request, the mock-up by email, adjustments by email. If you'd rather see each other, a video call is always possible.

Who owns my domain name?

You do. The domain is registered in your name; we manage it for you while you subscribe, but it remains your property.

How long am I committed for?

The subscription runs for 12 months. After that, you can cancel any month at no cost.

What happens if I stop?

Your domain stays yours and we hand over your website files so you can host it elsewhere. Subscription services (SMS reminders, review invitations, AI assistant) stop at the end of the last paid month.

Will you touch my email?

No. To connect the website to your domain, we only change the records that point to the website. Your email records stay as they are, so your mail keeps arriving as before.

What about data protection (Swiss FADP)?

Every website has its privacy policy, and we sign a data processing agreement covering your customers' data. Promotional SMS and WhatsApp messages only go to customers who have agreed to them; appointment reminders are allowed.

What do I need to provide?

Your basic texts (opening hours, menu or services, prices) and photos. No photos? An on-site shoot can be arranged from CHF 350 with a local photographer.
