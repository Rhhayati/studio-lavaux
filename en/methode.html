A short process, one point of contact.

Everything happens remotely, and from request to launch you deal with the same person. No project manager handing over to a designer handing over to a developer: the person who listens is the person who builds.

Five steps, all remote

  1. 2 minutes

    Online request

    You describe your business, your goals, your customers' languages and what you like. No meeting to schedule, no travel.

  2. 48 business hours

    Personalised mock-up

    We study your current website and Google profile, then send you a mock-up of your future site to view on your phone. Free, with no commitment.

  3. At your pace

    Approval and fine-tuning

    We refine it remotely, by email, or by video call if you prefer. You approve everything before it goes live.

  4. 7 days

    Launch

    Once we have your texts and photos, your website goes live within 7 days, connected to your domain and Google profile. If not, your first month's subscription is free.

  5. Then

    Support and automation

    We switch on the planned automations, make your changes and send you a regular report.

Our commitments

Your domain stays yours
It is registered in your name. We manage it while you subscribe, without ever owning it.
We never touch your email
To connect the website, only the records pointing to the website change. Your mailbox keeps working as before.
Fast hosting
Your website is served by a global delivery network, with a security certificate (HTTPS) and backups.
Swiss data protection
A privacy policy on every website, a data processing agreement for your customers' data, promotional messages only with their consent.
A reply within 24 business hours
A question, a change, a problem: you get an answer by the next business day at the latest.
On-time guarantee
If your website isn't live 7 days after we receive your content, your first month's subscription is free.

What we don't do

  • No travel required. Not for you, not for us: everything is handled remotely, and a video call is always an option.
  • No templates. Every website is designed for your business, starting from what makes it unique.
  • No hidden commitment. 12 months of subscription, in black and white, then cancellable monthly.
  • No fake or filtered reviews. We invite all your customers to share their opinion, as Google requires.
  • No promotional messages without consent. Appointment reminders yes, advertising only with the customer's agreement.
  • No public reply without you. Replies to your Google reviews and AI-drafted emails wait for your approval.
  • No lock-in. If you stop, your domain stays yours and you get your website files.
