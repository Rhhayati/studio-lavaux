Before you start

Is the mock-up really free, with no commitment?

Yes. You receive your personalised mock-up within 48 business hours without paying or signing anything. If it doesn't win you over, that's the end of it.

Do we need to meet?

No. Everything happens remotely: online request, mock-up by email, adjustments by email. A video call is possible if you'd like one.

How many languages can my website have?

French and one additional language are included with the Showcase Page and the Signature Website. Each further language costs CHF 290, once.

Which tools do you use?

Fast static websites hosted on the Cloudflare network, automation built with standard tools (n8n or Make), the WhatsApp Business API for messages and OpenAI models for the assistant. Nothing exotic: you are not tied to in-house software.

The ground rules

Commitment

12 months for the subscription, then cancellable monthly.

SMS and WhatsApp

Appointment reminders go to all your customers. Promotional messages only go to customers who have agreed to receive them, as Swiss unfair competition law requires. SMS volume is capped in the contract.

Data protection

A privacy policy on every website and a data processing agreement between us, in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection.

Your email

We never touch your email: only the records that point to the website are changed.
